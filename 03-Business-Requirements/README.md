# Business Goal

The objective of ShopEase is to provide customers with a secure, reliable, and user-friendly online shopping platform where they can browse products, add items to their cart, complete purchases, and manage their orders. The platform should also enable administrators to efficiently manage products, inventory, and customer orders.

---

# Business Objectives

- Increase online sales through an intuitive shopping experience.
- Improve customer satisfaction with a fast and reliable platform.
- Simplify the purchasing process.
- Reduce cart abandonment.
- Provide secure online payment processing.
- Enable efficient order and inventory management.

# Business Requirements

### User Management

- Customers shall be able to register a new account.
- Customers shall be able to log in using valid credentials.
- Customers shall be able to reset forgotten passwords.

### Product Catalogue

- Users shall be able to search products.
- Users shall be able to filter and sort products.
- Users shall be able to view detailed product information.

### Shopping Cart

- Users shall be able to add products to the cart.
- Users shall be able to remove products from the cart.
- Users shall be able to update product quantities.

### Checkout

- Users shall be able to enter shipping details.
- Users shall be able to select a payment method.
- Users shall receive an order confirmation after successful payment.

### User Account

- Users shall be able to update their profile.
- Users shall be able to view previous orders.

### Administration

- Administrators shall be able to add, edit, and delete products.
- Administrators shall be able to manage customer orders.
- Administrators shall be able to update inventory.

---

# Business Constraints

- The application must comply with data protection regulations.
- Payment transactions must be secure.
- The application must support modern web browsers.
- Downtime during business hours should be minimized.

---

# Assumptions

- Users have internet access.
- Users have a valid email address.
- Payment gateway services are available.
- Product inventory is maintained by administrators.

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- Users can successfully complete purchases.
- Payments are processed securely.
- Orders are recorded accurately.
- Customers can manage their accounts.
- Administrators can efficiently manage products and orders.
- Critical business workflows operate without high-severity defects.
